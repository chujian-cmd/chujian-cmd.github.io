How to Play

The Goal: Navigate from the top-left corner to the bottom-right corner.

The Twist: The maze is shrouded in fog! You can only see a small area around your current position.

  • Use arrow keys or WASD to move
  • On mobile, tap adjacent cells to move
  • Your trail fades behind you
  • Walls block your path - find the way through!
  • Reach the glowing exit to escape
