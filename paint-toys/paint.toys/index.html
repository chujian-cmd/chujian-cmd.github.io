Paint Toys

A small playground of creative canvases to bring out your creative side with a few beautiful limitations inspired by the art world!

Oil Paints
Dripping oils is a great way to explore the permanent and random nature of the art. This is the perfect place to get your Pollock on!
Sand in a Bottle
Layer colorful sand in a glass bottle. A relaxing particle simulation inspired by the classic craft.
Marbling
Drop ink onto water, drag a comb through. The swirly patterns you see inside the covers of old hardback books.
Symmetry Painter
Draw with magical mirrors multiplying your strokes across the canvas. Like drawing into a kaleidoscope.
Tessellation Painter
Draw in one tile and watch your pattern repeat across the entire canvas. Squares, triangles, or hexagons — fill the plane with your own repeating design.
Paint with Text
Combine poetry with brush strokes with this unique tool that can only really exist digitally. Calligrams are a great way to express yourself!
Paint like Mondrian
Are you a fan of abstract art? This Mondrian'esque art generator is a fun and accessible way to explore the world of neoplasticism and create your own modern masterpiece.
One Line
Sometimes limitations can bring out the most in your creativity, embrace your inner Picasso & try your hand at drawing with just a single line.
Etch-a-Sketch
Turn the knobs to draw, just like the toy. A canvas that favors the patient.
Zen Garden
Rake peaceful patterns in digital sand. A meditative drawing experience inspired by Japanese rock gardens.
Paint by Dots
Get your pointallism on... although I've made it a little easier to keep everything nice and spaced. How many dots can you place!
Scratch Art
Scratch away the black surface to reveal beautiful hidden colors underneath. Explore negative space.
String Art
Place pins, and weave strings between them, layer up to create depth and complexity. String art is tricky, but can be very beautiful.
Pixel Art
Draw with pixels in this retro art toy. Pixel art is a fun way to create. Embrace your inner mario brother.
Brushy Brush
What if you could draw with your drawings? Design your own brush shape, then use it to paint. Drop in an image, or sketch something weird. Your brush, your rules.
Paint and have Fun!
