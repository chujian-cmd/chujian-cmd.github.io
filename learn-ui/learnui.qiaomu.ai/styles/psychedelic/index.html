Psychedelic 迷幻风格（Psychedelic）

Psychedelic design is the visual language of the 1960s counterculture: saturated rainbow gradients, melting organic forms, Art Nouveau-inspired hand lettering, and optical patterns that seem to vibrate. It grew from concert posters for rock bands like the Grateful Dead and Jefferson Airplane, where artists borrowed Eastern mandalas, paisley, flower-power flora, and whiplash curves to evoke altered perception. The palette is bright and unapologetic - hot pinks, oranges, violets, and lime greens in flowing washes - and the mood is utopian, mystical, and handmade rather than dark or digital.

迷幻风格是 1960 年代反主流文化的视觉语言：饱和的彩虹渐变、融化的有机形态、受新艺术运动启发的手绘字体，以及看起来仿佛在振动的视错觉图案。它起源于 Grateful Dead、Jefferson Airplane 等摇滚乐队的演唱会海报，艺术家们借用东方曼陀罗、佩斯利花纹、花之力植物图案和鞭形曲线来唤起 altered perception。配色明亮而毫不妥协——热粉、橙、紫罗兰和青柠绿在流动的水洗色中层层叠加——整体气质是乌托邦式的、神秘的、手工艺的，而非黑暗或数字感的。

Scope: This entry covers the 1960s–70s psychedelic graphic style and its revivals in posters, album art, and UI. It does not cover the darker 1990s acid-house rave visual language (Acid Graphics) or the 2010s digital chrome-grid branch.

范围：本条覆盖 1960–70 年代迷幻平面风格及其在海报、唱片封面和 UI 中的复兴。不包含更黑暗的 1990 年代酸浩室锐舞视觉语言（Acid Graphics），也不包含 2010 年代数字铬金属网格分支。

Full style DNA完整风格 DNA

  1. Rainbow and saturated gradient washes彩虹与高饱和渐变水洗 color defining定义特征

    Color moves in full-spectrum arcs - red-to-violet gradients, hot pink-to-orange-to-yellow blends, and saturated purple/green complements - applied as backgrounds, type fills, or aura glows.

    颜色以全光谱弧线流动——红到紫罗兰渐变、热粉到橙到黄的混合、高饱和的紫/绿互补色——用作背景、字体填充或光晕。

  2. Organic flowing and biomorphic forms有机流动与生物形态 geometry defining定义特征

    Shapes melt, swirl, and bloom like liquid or smoke - no straight edges or precise grids; contours recall Art Nouveau vines, paisley teardrops, and amoeba-like blobs.

    形状像液体或烟雾一样融化、旋转、绽放——没有直边或精确网格；轮廓让人想起新艺术藤蔓、佩斯利泪滴和变形虫般的 blob。

  3. Art Nouveau-inspired hand lettering新艺术风格手绘字体 typography defining定义特征

    Letterforms are warped, wavy, or stretched into decorative curves with heavy outlines and rainbow fills, often reading as hand-drawn poster lettering rather than clean type.

    字母被扭曲、波浪化或拉伸成装饰性曲线，带粗重轮廓和彩虹填充，读起来更像是手绘海报字体，而非干净的印刷字体。

  4. Optical illusion and moire patterns视错觉与摩尔纹图案 surface defining定义特征

    Concentric rings, repeating radiating lines, moire overlays, and vibrating color combinations create a sense of movement or depth without animation.

    同心圆环、重复放射线、摩尔纹叠加和振动配色组合在不借助动画的情况下营造出运动感或纵深感。

  5. Mandalas, paisley, and flower-power flora曼陀罗、佩斯利与花之力植物 imagery defining定义特征

    Decorative motifs include circular mandalas, paisley droplets, daisies, mushrooms, and stylized eyes - imagery borrowed from Eastern spirituality and hippie symbolism.

    装饰母题包括圆形曼陀罗、佩斯利水滴、雏菊、蘑菇和风格化眼睛——借自东方灵性与嬉皮士符号系统。

  6. Dense maximal layering密集极繁层叠 layout supporting辅助特征

    Type, ornamental borders, flora, and radiant patterns overlap in busy compositions with a horror vacui impulse, though usually lighter than Acid Graphics.

    字体、装饰边框、植物图案和放射状纹理在繁忙构图中相互重叠，带有「恐惧留白」的冲动，不过通常比 Acid Graphics 更明亮。

  7. Light or fully saturated grounds明亮或全饱和底色 color supporting辅助特征

    Backgrounds are often bright yellow, hot pink, orange, or rainbow gradients rather than the near-black grounds of later digital psychedelia.

    背景通常是明黄、热粉、橙色或彩虹渐变，而非后期数字迷幻那种近黑色地面。

  8. Dark chrome and wireframe grids黑暗铬金属与线框网格 surface avoid避免

    Black backgrounds, liquid mercury lettering, and warped wireframe grids belong to Acid Graphics; their presence pulls the style away from 1960s psychedelia.

    黑色背景、液态水银字体和扭曲线框网格属于 Acid Graphics；它们一出现，风格就会偏离 1960 年代迷幻。

Acid Graphics酸性图形（Acid Graphics）

This is Psychedelic because the forms are organic, hand-drawn, and flowing - rainbow gradients, paisley, mandalas, and Art Nouveau curves on light or saturated grounds - rather than the dark, digital, chrome-and-wireframe rave language of Acid Graphics.

这是迷幻风格，因为它的形态是有机的、手绘的、流动的——彩虹渐变、佩斯利花纹、曼陀罗和新艺术曲线铺在明亮或高饱和的底色上，而不是 Acid Graphics 那种黑暗的、数字化的铬金属与线框网格锐舞语言。

It would become Acid Graphics if the background turned near-black, the colors shifted to toxic neon green and magenta, the organic curves were replaced by melting chrome 3D type and warped wireframe grids, and the mood moved from 1960s counterculture to underground club culture.

如果背景变成近黑色、颜色变成有毒的荧光绿和品红、有机曲线被融化的铬金属 3D 字体和扭曲的线框网格取代，整体气质从 60 年代反主流文化转向地下俱乐部文化，那就变成 Acid Graphics 了。

Style brief — paste into your agent风格 Brief —— 粘贴给你的代理

Create the surface using Psychedelic design. Defining signals: a bright saturated ground - hot pink (#ff2a6d), tangerine (#ffae00), electric violet (#9d4edd), lime (#ccff00) - blended in flowing rainbow gradients; organic biomorphic shapes that melt and swirl without straight edges; Art Nouveau-inspired hand lettering with wavy, stretched forms and heavy decorative outlines; optical-illusion patterns such as concentric rings, radiating lines, and moire overlays that create vibration without animation; mandalas, paisley teardrops, daisies, mushrooms, and stylized eyes as ornamental motifs. Keep specific flora and color stops flexible. Use a bold retro sans-serif such as `Impact, Haettenschweiler, 'Arial Narrow Bold', sans-serif` for display type; set wavy rainbow fills with `background-clip: text` plus a dark outline (`-webkit-text-stroke: 1px #2a0a31`). Avoid Acid Graphics: if the background turns near-black, the colors shift to toxic neon green/magenta, and chrome 3D lettering with warped wireframe grids appears, you have crossed into the rave-digital cousin. Keep functional labels on solid light panels (#fff8e7 or #ffe4ec) rather than over vibrating patterns, provide visible focus states, and disable slow-drift or spin animations under prefers-reduced-motion.

用迷幻风格（Psychedelic）创建这个界面。决定性信号：明亮高饱和的底色——热粉（#ff2a6d）、橘黄（#ffae00）、电光紫（#9d4edd）、青柠绿（#ccff00）——在流动的彩虹渐变中融合；有机生物形态，融化旋转而没有直边；受新艺术运动启发的手绘字体，字母波浪化、拉伸并带粗重装饰轮廓；视错觉图案，如同心圆环、放射线和摩尔纹叠加，不借助动画就产生振动感；曼陀罗、佩斯利水滴、雏菊、蘑菇和风格化眼睛作为装饰母题。具体植物和色阶保持灵活。展示字体用粗重的复古无衬线，如 `Impact, Haettenschweiler, 'Arial Narrow Bold', sans-serif`；彩虹填充用 `background-clip: text`，再加深色描边（`-webkit-text-stroke: 1px #2a0a31`）。不要滑向 Acid Graphics：如果背景变近黑、颜色变成有毒荧光绿/品红、出现铬金属 3D 字体和扭曲线框网格，你就跨进了锐舞数字亲戚。功能文字放在纯色浅面板（#fff8e7 或 #ffe4ec）上，不要压在振动图案上；提供可见焦点状态；在 prefers-reduced-motion 下关闭慢速漂移或旋转动画。

Accessibility & misuse可访问性与误用

Psychedelic palettes are bright in hue but often fail value contrast: rainbow text over rainbow grounds can drop below 2:1. Keep functional labels on solid light panels (#fff8e7, #ffe4ec) with dark ink, and treat the vibrating patterns and rainbow type as decorative only.

迷幻配色在色相上很亮，但明度对比常常不及格：彩虹字压在彩虹底上可能降到 2:1 以下。功能标签要放在纯色浅面板（#fff8e7、#ffe4ec）上配深色字，把振动图案和彩虹字体只当作装饰。

Slowly rotating mandalas, pulsing auras, and drifting moire can trigger vestibular discomfort or photosensitive responses; respect prefers-reduced-motion by freezing or simplifying motion, and keep any flashing below the WCAG three-flashes-per-second threshold.

缓慢旋转的曼陀罗、脉动的光晕和漂移的摩尔纹可能诱发前庭不适或光敏反应；尊重 prefers-reduced-motion，冻结或简化动画，并确保任何闪烁低于 WCAG 每秒三次闪烁阈值。

Dense ornamental layering and distorted letterforms hurt readability and cognitive parsing; reserve psychedelic effects for headings, hero art, and decorative borders, and keep body text in a plain sans-serif at a comfortable size with clear hierarchy.

密集的装饰层叠和扭曲字母会损害可读性和认知解析；把迷幻效果留给标题、主视觉艺术和装饰边框，正文保持用普通无衬线、舒适字号和清晰层级。

Origin起源

Psychedelic art emerged in the mid-1960s alongside the counterculture and psychedelic rock scenes of San Francisco and London. In the United States, the so-called 'Big Five' of poster art - Wes Wilson, Victor Moscoso, Rick Griffin, Stanley Mouse, and Alton Kelley - defined the look for venues such as the Fillmore Auditorium and the Avalon Ballroom, drawing on Art Nouveau, Indian miniatures, and Op Art. British artist Martin Sharp produced iconic posters for Cream and Jimi Hendrix, while Peter Max carried the style into broader pop graphics and commercial licensing in the late 1960s and 1970s. The style faded with the end of the hippie era but revives whenever design wants to signal altered perception, utopianism, or handmade excess.

迷幻艺术于 1960 年代中期与旧金山和伦敦的反主流文化、迷幻摇滚场景同时出现。在美国，所谓海报艺术「五巨头」—— Wes Wilson、Victor Moscoso、Rick Griffin、Stanley Mouse 和 Alton Kelley —— 为 Fillmore Auditorium、Avalon Ballroom 等场地奠定了视觉语言，借鉴新艺术运动、印度细密画和欧普艺术。英国艺术家 Martin Sharp 为 Cream 和 Jimi Hendrix 创作了标志性海报，而 Peter Max 在 1960 年代末到 1970 年代把这种风格带入更广泛的流行图形和商业授权。嬉皮士时代结束后它逐渐消退，但每当设计想表达 altered perception、乌托邦主义或手工过剩时，它就会复兴。
