Hand-drawn / Doodle手绘涂鸦（Hand-drawn / Doodle）

This is Chalkboard because…这是 Chalkboard，因为……

This is Chalkboard because the ground is a dark slate board and every mark is chalk: white or pastel strokes with grainy edges, doubled hand-lettering, dust, and eraser smudges.

这是黑板粉笔风格，因为底是深石板色的板面，每一笔都是粉笔：白色或彩色笔画带颗粒边缘、描着重影的手写字、粉尘和板擦擦痕。

It would become Hand-drawn / Doodle if…如果……就变成 Hand-drawn / Doodle 了

It would become Hand-drawn / Doodle if the board were swapped for light paper and the chalk for pen or marker — dark ink on a cream ground, with no dust, no smudges, and no pastel chalk.

如果把板面换成浅色纸张、粉笔换成钢笔或马克笔 —— 米色底上的深色墨迹，没有粉尘、没有擦痕、没有彩色粉笔 —— 就变成手绘涂鸦了。
