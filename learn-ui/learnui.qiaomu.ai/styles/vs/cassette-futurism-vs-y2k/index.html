Y2K Digital AestheticY2K 数字美学

This is Cassette Futurism because…这是 Cassette Futurism，因为……

This is Cassette Futurism because the future is built from analog media — CRT scanlines, phosphor glow, cassette chrome, VHS artifacts, and wireframe vector graphics on low-res screens — not from the polished digital chrome and rendered 3D of the millennium.

这是磁带未来主义，因为它的未来由模拟媒介搭建 —— CRT 扫描线、荧光辉光、磁带铬色、VHS 伪影和低分辨率屏幕上的线框矢量图形 —— 而不是千禧年那种打磨过的数字铬和 3D 渲染。

It would become Y2K Digital Aesthetic if…如果……就变成 Y2K Digital Aesthetic 了

It would become Y2K if the analog hardware and tape decay were replaced by liquid chrome, gel plastic, iridescent blue-silver, rendered orbs, and wide techno type as if the future had been rendered by early-2000s CGI.

如果把模拟硬件和磁带磨损换成液态铬、凝胶塑料、幻彩蓝银、渲染球体和宽体科技字体，仿佛未来是由 2000 年代初的 CGI 渲染出来的，就变成 Y2K 数字美学了。
