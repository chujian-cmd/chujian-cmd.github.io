Solarpunk 太阳朋克（Solarpunk）

Solarpunk answers cyberpunk's neon dystopia with a sun-drenched, botanical future: interfaces feel like open-air greenhouses, solar arrays, and stained-glass market halls built from bamboo, clay, and recycled glass. The palette leans on warm terracotta, sage green, solar gold, sky cyan, and cream; geometry borrows from Art Nouveau vines, Islamic tilework, and hexagonal solar cells rather than hard chrome grids. It is optimistic infrastructure made visible — wind turbines, leaves, sun rays, and water channels rendered as decorative but functional UI patterns.

太阳朋克用阳光浸透、植物繁茂的未来回应赛博朋克的霓虹反乌托邦：界面感觉像开放式温室、太阳能阵列，以及由竹子、黏土和再生玻璃建成的彩色玻璃市集大厅。配色偏向暖陶土色、鼠尾草绿、太阳能金、天空青和奶油白；几何语言借鉴新艺术风格的藤蔓、伊斯兰瓷砖纹样和六边形太阳能电池，而非硬朗的铬合金网格。它让乐观的基础设施变得可见——风车、叶片、阳光和水渠被渲染成装饰性的功能 UI 图案。

Scope: This entry covers the visual/UI language of solarpunk — renewable, botanical, and craft-future surfaces. It overlaps with Art Nouveau and Art Deco ornament but is distinct in its ecological optimism and solar/craft technology motifs; it is also distinct from biopunk, which treats biology as wet, medical, and often dystopian.

范围：本条覆盖太阳朋克的视觉与界面语言——可再生、植物性与工艺未来表面。它与新艺术风格（Art Nouveau）和装饰艺术（Art Deco）的纹样有交集，但区别在于生态乐观主义以及太阳能/工艺技术母题；它也与生物朋克（Biopunk）不同，后者把生物学表现为潮湿、医疗化且常带反乌托邦色彩。

Full style DNA完整风格 DNA

  1. Solar and botanical palette太阳能与植物配色 color defining定义特征

    Dominant hues are warm terracotta (#d97736), sage green (#84a98c), solar gold (#f5b800), sky cyan (#5ec9e8), and cream (#faf6ec), with accents of leaf green (#4a7c59) and ochre earth (#c4884a).

    主色调是暖陶土色（#d97736）、鼠尾草绿（#84a98c）、太阳能金（#f5b800）、天空青（#5ec9e8）和奶油白（#faf6ec），点缀叶绿色（#4a7c59）和赭石大地色（#c4884a）。

  2. Living, renewable geometry有机的可再生几何 geometry defining定义特征

    Shapes combine hexagonal solar cells, radiating sun rays, vine-like curves, and arched greenhouse windows — geometry that looks grown or assembled by hand rather than machined.

    形状组合六边形太阳能电池、放射状阳光、藤蔓曲线和拱形温室窗——几何看起来是长出来的或手工组装的，而不是机器切削的。

  3. Botanical and solar ornament植物与太阳能装饰 imagery defining定义特征

    Leaves, vines, sunbursts, wind turbines, water channels, and stained-glass motifs appear as patterns, icons, and container frames; decoration doubles as functional data visualization.

    叶片、藤蔓、阳光放射、风车、水渠和彩色玻璃纹样作为图案、图标和容器边框反复出现；装饰同时承担数据可视化功能。

  4. Craft and earth surfaces工艺与大地材质 surface defining定义特征

    Surfaces suggest terracotta, woven bamboo, unbleached linen, recycled paper, and colored glass — matte or softly textured, never cold chrome or slick plastic.

    表面暗示陶土、编织竹、未漂白亚麻、再生纸和彩色玻璃——哑光或柔和纹理，绝不会是冷铬或亮面塑料。

  5. Generous natural light充沛的自然光 depth supporting辅助特征

    Soft warm shadows, golden rim light, and sun-ray gradients replace neon glow and hard metallic specularity; depth comes from layered translucency and warm illumination.

    柔和的暖阴影、金色轮廓光和阳光射线渐变取代霓虹辉光和硬金属高光；纵深来自层叠的半透明与温暖照明。

  6. Arched and rounded containers拱形与圆角容器 layout supporting辅助特征

    Cards, buttons, and windows favor generous rounded corners, arches, and pill shapes — like greenhouse windows, planters, and clay tiles — rather than sharp corners or perfect rectangles.

    卡片、按钮和窗口偏爱大方的圆角、拱形和胶囊形——像温室窗、花盆和黏土瓦——而不是尖角或完美矩形。

  7. Dystopian chrome and neon反乌托邦铬合金与霓虹 color avoid避免

    Rain-slicked black streets, hot magenta/cyan neon, and hard metallic grids push the look toward cyberpunk or chrome sci-fi; solarpunk keeps the night sky starlit, not neon-lit.

    雨水浸泡的黑色街道、热品红/电光青霓虹和硬金属网格会把外观推向赛博朋克或铬合金科幻；太阳朋克让夜空布满星光，而不是霓虹。

  8. Clinical wet biology临床湿生物学 surface avoid避免

    Viscous gels, mucus textures, petri dishes, and vascular patterns are the signature of biopunk; solarpunk treats biology as dry, planted, and cultivated.

    黏稠凝胶、黏液质感、培养皿和血管图案是生物朋克的标志；太阳朋克把生物学表现为干燥、种植和栽培的。

Biopunk生物朋克（Biopunk）

This is solarpunk because the organic world is sunlit, cultivated, and architectural — plants, terracotta, glass, and solar geometry — rather than wet, clinical, or bodily like petri dishes, mucus, and vascular tissue.

这是太阳朋克，因为它的有机世界是阳光充足、被栽培且建筑化的——植物、陶土、玻璃和太阳能几何——而不是像培养皿、黏液和血管组织那样潮湿、临床化或身体化。

It would become biopunk if the greenhouse turned into a containment lab, the terracotta cooled to sterile grey, the palette flipped to sickly greens and bruised pinks, and the vines were replaced by throbbing membranes and gel cultures.

如果温室变成隔离实验室，陶土变冷成无菌灰，配色翻成病态绿和瘀青粉，藤蔓被搏动的薄膜和凝胶培养物取代，它就变成生物朋克了。

Style brief — paste into your agent风格 Brief —— 粘贴给你的代理

Build the interface as Solarpunk. Canvas: warm parchment (#faf6ec) or sun-washed clay (#f3e6d3), with plenty of breathable whitespace. Defining signals: palette anchored in warm terracotta (#d97736), sage green (#84a98c), solar gold (#f5b800), sky cyan (#5ec9e8), and cream (#faf6ec); geometry combines hexagonal solar cells, radiating sun rays, vine-like curves, and arched greenhouse windows; surfaces read as terracotta, bamboo, linen, and colored glass — matte and craft-like, never chrome. Use generous rounded corners (22px cards, 999px pills), soft warm shadows, and golden rim-light. Buttons: linear-gradient(180deg, #e08c52, #d97736, #b85c26) with inset top highlight and terracotta shadow. Progress bars: repeating-conic-gradient sun rays in #f5b800 / #fbd24d over a sage track (#dce8d9), with a soft glow. Typography: warm humanist sans (13–15px titles, 11–12px data) or a craft-style rounded sans; labels in deep bark brown (#3d2314). Imagery: leaves, vines, sunbursts, turbines, water channels, stained-glass motifs. Do not drift into biopunk or cyberpunk: avoid sickly greens, mucus textures, neon magenta-cyan, rain-slicked metal, and dystopian darkness. Ensure text contrast (bark brown #3d2314 on cream #faf6ec passes easily; gold #f5b800 only as accent, not body text), visible focus rings, and honor prefers-reduced-motion by stopping rotating sun rays.

按太阳朋克（Solarpunk）构建这个界面。画布：温暖的羊皮纸色（#faf6ec）或日晒黏土色（#f3e6d3），留出大量透气留白。决定性信号：配色锚定在暖陶土色（#d97736）、鼠尾草绿（#84a98c）、太阳能金（#f5b800）、天空青（#5ec9e8）和奶油白（#faf6ec）；几何结合六边形太阳能电池、放射阳光、藤蔓曲线和拱形温室窗；表面读起来像陶土、竹子、亚麻和彩色玻璃——哑光且手工艺感，绝无铬合金。使用大方的圆角（卡片 22px、胶囊 999px）、柔和暖阴影和金色轮廓光。按钮：linear-gradient(180deg, #e08c52, #d97736, #b85c26)，带顶部内高光和陶土阴影。进度条：#f5b800 / #fbd24d 的重复圆锥渐变阳光射线，铺在鼠尾草轨道（#dce8d9）上，带柔和光晕。字体：温暖的人文主义无衬线（标题 13–15px、数据 11–12px）或工艺感圆角无衬线；标签用深树皮棕（#3d2314）。图像：叶片、藤蔓、阳光放射、风车、水渠、彩色玻璃纹样。不要滑向生物朋克或赛博朋克：避免病态绿、黏液质感、霓虹品红-电光青、雨湿金属和反乌托邦黑暗。保证文字对比度（树皮棕 #3d2314 在奶油白 #faf6ec 上轻松通过；太阳能金 #f5b800 只作点缀，不用作正文），焦点状态可见，并在 prefers-reduced-motion 下停止旋转阳光。

Accessibility & misuse可访问性与误用

Warm golds and terracottas are atmospheric but can fail 4.5:1 against parchment if used for small body text — keep functional text in bark brown (#3d2314) on cream (#faf6ec) and reserve solar gold (#f5b800) for icons, accents, and large display type only.

暖金色和陶土色很有氛围，但用作小号正文时可能在羊皮纸背景上达不到 4.5:1——功能文字保留树皮棕（#3d2314）在奶油白（#faf6ec）上，太阳能金（#f5b800）只用于图标、点缀和大号展示字。

Rotating sun rays, drifting leaves, and pulsing solar animations can trigger vestibular issues; make them decorative and slow, and pause them under prefers-reduced-motion.

旋转阳光、飘动叶片和脉冲太阳能动画可能诱发前庭问题；保持它们装饰性且缓慢，并在 prefers-reduced-motion 下暂停。

Ornate botanical patterns and stained-glass backgrounds raise visual noise: keep primary actions on clean clay/parchment cards, avoid placing text directly over busy floral motifs, and never rely on green/gold color alone to indicate status.

华丽的植物图案和彩色玻璃背景会增加视觉噪声：主操作放在干净的黏土/羊皮纸卡片上，避免把文字直接压在繁复花卉纹样上，且不要只靠绿色/金色传达状态。

Origin起源

Solarpunk emerged in the late 2000s as an online counter-proposal to cyberpunk's technological pessimism, crystallizing around 2014 in art, fiction, and design communities that asked what a sustainable, equitable future could look like. It drew together Art Nouveau's organic line work, 1970s ecological utopianism, and contemporary renewable-energy iconography — solar panels, wind turbines, green roofs, and community gardens. As a visual style it became a recognizable strand of climate-optimistic concept art, album covers, and independent games, defined less by a single studio than by a shared aesthetic brief: nature and technology reconciled under sunlight.

太阳朋克在 2000 年代末作为对赛博朋克技术悲观主义的在线反提案出现，并在 2014 年前后于艺术、小说和设计社区中结晶为一群人追问「可持续、公正的未来会是什么样子」。它融合新艺术风格的有机线条、1970 年代生态乌托邦主义，以及当代可再生能源图像——太阳能板、风力发电机、绿色屋顶和社区菜园。作为视觉风格，它成为气候乐观主义概念艺术、专辑封面和独立游戏中可识别的一支，其定义更多来自一份共享美学简报而非某家单一工作室：自然与技术在阳光下和解。
