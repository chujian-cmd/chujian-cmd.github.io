frequently asked questions

how do i win?

each toy has a win condition. complete it to check it off the scoreboard.

tictactoe

beat the ai (get 3 in a row). the ai is beatable - it makes mistakes.

snake

eat 10 food. use wasd or arrow keys. click to focus.

pong

score 5 points against the ai. move your paddle with the mouse.

checkers

capture all ai pieces. the ai is beatable - it blunders sometimes. click piece, then destination.

breakout

destroy all bricks. move paddle with mouse. you have 3 lives.

minesweeper

reveal all non-mine cells. right-click to flag. 6 mines on a 6x6 grid.

flappy ant

pass 10 pipes. click or tap to flap.

whackamole

whack 10 moles before 3 escape.

simon

repeat a 10-color sequence. watch, then click the quadrants in order.

memory

match all 8 pairs without mistakes. a mistake = clicking a card you've seen that isn't the match.

connect 4

drop discs to get 4 in a row. beat the ai. click a column to drop.

jumping ant

bounce upward on platforms. move mouse or touch to steer. reach height 100.

ants

a secret game. figure it out.

what happens when i win everything?

if the games themselves aren't reward enough, there's still a little something.
